# Comprehensive Walkthrough Script: Master the Agency OS

**Duration:** 8-10 Minutes
**Goal:** In-depth technical walkthrough of Agency Nexus, detailing initial configuration, licensing, multi-provider AI setup, and step-by-step module flows for admins and teams.

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 1:00)

**Visual:** Show the presenter on camera or a screen recording of a beautiful, live WordPress site. Open the WordPress admin dashboard and hover over the **Nexus** sidebar icon.

**Voiceover:**
"Hey everyone! Today, we’re going to walk through the initial setup, configuration, and daily operations of **Agency Nexus**—the first all-in-one Agency Operating System built directly on WordPress. We’re going to look at how to centralize your leads, projects, messaging, and financials, and how to configure and utilize our native **AI Copilot Engine** to automate your agency's administrative burden. Let’s dive in!"

---

## 2. Core & AI Configuration (1:00 - 2:30)

**Visual:** Click on **Nexus > Settings**. Scroll to "AI Copilot Configuration." Turn the global toggle "Enable AI Suite" on. Choose "OpenAI" as the active provider from the dropdown. Show the API key input fields (`an_openai_key`, `an_gemini_key`, `an_claude_key`). Enter a demo key, choose `gpt-4o` as the model, and hit Save.

**Voiceover:**
"Once you activate Agency Nexus, your first stop is **Nexus > Settings**. Here you'll upload your agency logo and set your hourly rates.
Next, scroll to the **AI Copilot** tab. This is the brain of the ecosystem. It supports multiple LLM providers natively: OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude. Simply toggle the suite on, choose your active provider, enter your API key, and select your preferred model.
And if you don't have an external API key configured? Don't worry! Agency Nexus features a built-in, context-aware **Local CoPilot engine** that runs unconditionally on the client-side by default, allowing you to use content polishing and smart schedules right out of the box."

---

## 3. Lead Capture & AI Scoring (2:30 - 4:00)

**Visual:** Click into **Nexus > Leads**. Show the "Generate Embed Code" button. Navigate to a frontend page with an embedded form. Fill out the form as a dummy lead with details like "We need a massive technical SEO campaign for our e-commerce brand, budget $10k." Submit the form, then go back to the admin Leads dashboard to show the new lead.

**Voiceover:**
"Now let's look at the lead capture flow. You can generate an embed code with a single click and place it on your marketing site. When a prospect submits their details, they flow directly into your secure database under **Nexus > Leads**, preserving UTM tracking parameters.
But notice what happens next: the system automatically scores the lead from 0-100 based on value, and our AI Copilot parses their submission to generate a concise, strategic executive summary, saving your sales team from manual review."

---

## 4. Smart Scoping & AI Proposals (4:00 - 5:30)

**Visual:** Select the lead, click "Convert to Client." Navigate to **Nexus > Proposals**, click "New Proposal." Open the Scope Builder. Choose "SEO Campaign," toggle several addons (Technical Audit, Backlink Outreach), showing the budget update. Click the **✨ AI Rewrite** button, showing the raw scope items turn into polished, persuasive deliverables copy. Click "Generate Proposal."

**Voiceover:**
"Once your lead is ready, convert them to a Client with one click, which automatically sets up their WordPress portal account.
Next, open the **Scope Builder** to draft your proposal. Toggle base services and addons to build a dynamic scope.
Before sending, click this **✨ AI Rewrite** button. The AI Copilot takes your raw scope points and instantly drafts a professional, high-ticket proposal detailing your exact objectives and deliverables. Click send, and your client receives a secure, branded, e-signable proposal in their portal."

---

## 5. Execution, AI SOPs & True ROI (5:30 - 7:30)

**Visual:** Navigate to **Nexus > Projects**. Open an active project. Add a task called "On-Page SEO Optimization." Click the **🪄 Generate AI Brief** button. Watch as the task description is populated with a step-by-step procedural brief. Log some dummy time against the task. Navigate to the project's Financial tab to show the "True ROI" calculations updating.

**Voiceover:**
"Once your proposal is e-signed, AutoPilot can trigger a project kickoff. Under **Nexus > Projects**, you can map out tasks and dependencies.
To save time during delegation, click the **Generate AI Brief** button when adding a task. The AI Copilot instantly writes a complete, step-by-step Standard Operating Procedure for that task, ensuring your team has a perfect roadmap.
As your team logs hours against these tasks, our **MoneyFlow** engine subtracts their labor cost from the project budget, showing you your True Project Profitability in real-time."

---

## 6. Communication & AI Message Polish (7:30 - 9:00)

**Visual:** Navigate to **Nexus > Messaging**. Open a chat thread with a client. Draft a rough message: "we finished the audit but the links are broken we are fixing it now." Click **✨ AI Polish**. The draft instantly changes to: "We have completed the technical audit of your site. Our team has identified a few temporary link redirects that we are currently resolving. We will share the final report shortly." Send the message. Show the file repository.

**Voiceover:**
"We keep all project communication inside the client portal to eliminate email confusion. Under **Nexus > Messages**, you can chat securely with clients.
If you are drafting a quick response and want to make sure it sounds professional, click **✨ AI Polish**. The Copilot rewrites your rough draft into a highly polished, client-facing message in seconds. You can also view and manage version-controlled files inside the Shared Repository, giving your clients absolute clarity."

---

## 7. Automated Operations & Reminders (9:00 - END)

**Visual:** Go to **Nexus > Settings > AutoPilot**. Show a rule: "If Invoice Overdue -> Draft AI Reminder." Navigate to **Nexus > Invoices**, show an unpaid invoice, click **🪄 Draft AI Reminder**, and show the beautifully drafted email draft. Go to the dashboard, showing the "Daily Briefing" widget.

**Voiceover:**
"Finally, let's look at automation. Under **AutoPilot**, you can configure rules to run your agency on cruise control.
For unpaid invoices, navigate to **Invoices** and click **Draft AI Reminder**. The AI analyzes the invoice context and previous client interactions to draft a polite overdue notification.
Every morning, the system sends an AI-summarized **Daily Briefing** email to the administrator, highlighting overdue tasks, fresh leads, and urgent social interactions.
That’s it! You have successfully calibrated your agency. Stop managing tools—start scaling your agency with Agency Nexus."

**Visual:** Presenter on camera or a final brand slide with the CTA text and website URL.
