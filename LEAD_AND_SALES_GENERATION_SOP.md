# SOP: High-Ticket Lead & Sales Generation Playbook (Agency OS)

**Goal:** Establish a standardized, highly automated, and predictable pipeline to attract, nurture, qualify, and close high-ticket clients utilizing the full power of the **Agency Nexus** ecosystem.

**Scope:** Applies to agency owners, sales development representatives (SDRs), and marketing personnel.

---

## 🏗 STAGE 1: Top-of-Funnel Inbound Traffic (ContentMatrix Authority Clusters)

This stage outlines how to build an inbound engine that positions your agency as a primary authority, capturing organic search intent.

### 1. Topic Cluster Mapping (Pillar Content Architect)
*   **Step 1:** Navigate to **Nexus > ContentMatrix > Pillar Architect**.
*   **Step 2:** Define your core service pillars (e.g. "Scale My Agency", "Client Onboarding SOP").
*   **Step 3:** Generate 5-10 surrounding "Cluster" sub-topics targeting key search-intent questions.
*   **Step 4:** Visually link each Cluster draft back to its primary Pillar to build a strong internal linking network.

### 2. SEO-Optimized Keyword Drafting (AI Content Refiner)
*   **Step 1:** Create draft titles based on target SEO keyword gaps using the **Batch Automation** tool.
*   **Step 2:** Click the **✨ AI Improve Title** link next to the Title field. The AI will output a catchy, high-CTR headline limited to 60 characters to prevent SERP truncation.
*   **Step 3:** Click the **✨ AI Improve Content** link next to the Body Content field. The AI will rewrite your raw draft into a beautifully structured, keyword-optimized SEO article complete with appropriate headings (`H2`, `H3`), lists, paragraphs, and a compelling Call to Action (CTA).
*   **Step 4:** Drag and drop the finished draft on the **Visual Calendar** to schedule its publishing.

---

## 🧠 STAGE 2: Mid-of-Funnel Qualification & Social Listening (EngageTrack CRM)

This stage governs lead capture and social qualification, ensuring your sales team focuses only on high-value prospects.

### 1. Smart Capture & High-Converting Forms
*   **Step 1:** Embed the `[an_lead_form]` shortcode on high-traffic landing pages.
*   **Step 2:** Configure the Form Redirect Builder to send successfully completed leads directly to a high-converting Thank You page (using `[agency_nexus_thank_you]`) to maximize downstream engagement.

### 2. CRM Lead Scoring & Summarization
*   **Step 3:** Check the **EngageTrack Leads Dashboard** daily at 9:00 AM.
*   **Step 4:** Sort leads by **Lead Score**. Ranks over 70 indicate high-intent, premium buyers.
*   **Step 5:** Read the **AI-generated Lead Summary** describing their business and operational bottlenecks. This provides critical, context-rich talking points before booking a call.

### 3. Sentiment Listening & Intent Tracking (Social Hub)
*   **Step 6:** Open the **EngageTrack Social Hub** to monitor mentions and interactions across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X.
*   **Step 7:** Focus on comments and queries flagged with negative sentiment or high buying intent. Click **✨ AI Suggest Response** (which automatically uses the selected default provider in Settings) to draft highly engaging, professional replies to capture their interest and redirect them to book a discovery call.

---

## 💼 STAGE 3: Bottom-of-Funnel Collaborative Closing (SmartOnboard Scope Builder)

This stage guides the sales call and proposal generation, using transparent, dynamic pricing to close the deal on the spot.

### 1. Live Interactive Scoping (Collaborative Pricing)
*   **Step 1:** Open the **SmartOnboard Scope Builder** live on a Zoom/consultation call with the prospect.
*   **Step 2:** Select the base service type (e.g. SEO Strategy, Web Design) and choose the appropriate Project Scale tier (Small, Medium, Large).
*   **Step 3:** Ask the client which add-ons they require (e.g. Express Delivery, Strategy Consultation, Support), and check the boxes in real-time. The **Dynamic Total** updates instantly, establishing collaborative transparency.

### 2. Applying Discounts & Exact Computation
*   **Step 4:** If a discount is offered, enter it in the **Special Terms / Discounts** field.
    *   *Percentage:* e.g. "10% VIP Discount"
    *   *Flat:* e.g. "$500 loyalty coupon"
*   **Step 5:** Click **Generate Proposal**. The Scope Builder automatically parses the discount, subtracts it from the subtotal exactly, and writes a detailed pricing computation breakdown directly into the description.
*   **Step 6:** Use **✨ AI Rewrite** on the proposal view to polish rough deliverables into highly persuasive, high-ticket scope objectives.

### 3. E-Sign & Conversion
*   **Step 7:** Direct the client to the frontend **Approval Portal** to review the exact pricing computation and digitally sign the proposal to finalize the onboarding process.

---

## 🤖 STAGE 4: Post-onboarding Delivery Automation (AutoPilot & ClientSync)

This stage automates administrative handoffs, allowing you to scale with zero tool friction.

### 1. Event-Trigger Kickoffs
*   **Step 1:** Enable the AutoPilot trigger: `If Proposal Signed -> Create Project`.
*   **Step 2:** The system will automatically register the Client User account, spin up the Project with the exact computed Scope budget in MoneyFlow, and notify the Project Lead via Slack.

### 2. Secure Project Workspace (ClientSync)
*   **Step 3:** Log in to **ClientSync Messages** and upload standard deliverables or wireframes to the secure Shared Vault, ensuring strict role-based client-staff visibility.
*   **Step 4:** Monitor progress on the **Gantt Chart**, logging labor hours to keep margins and True ROI healthy and profitable.

---
**CALIBRATION METRICS TO WATCH (Weekly):**
- **Inbound Lead Volume:** Total leads captured via EngageTrack form shortcodes.
- **Proposal Close Rate:** Percentage of generated Scope Builder proposals signed and paid.
- **True ROI Margin:** Average profit margins calculated dynamically by MoneyFlow.
