Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the WordPress-native Agency OS.

What is a WordPress-native Agency OS? +
Unlike expensive monthly SaaS systems (Asana, ClickUp, HubSpot) that live on external servers and track your data, Agency Nexus is a plugin that installs directly on your WordPress website. This means you own your data 100%, pay zero monthly "tab taxes", and can fully white-label the entire portal experience for your clients.
How does the Scope Builder Pricing Computation work? +
The Scope Builder is mathematically precise. You select a base scale budget (Small/Medium/Large) and toggle your service add-ons. You can input flat or percentage-based discounts directly into the "Special Terms" field, and the system automatically calculates the subtotal, subtracts the discount exactly, and creates a clean pricing breakdown inside the client proposal description.
Can I use my own AI Provider? +
Yes! Under Settings, you can enable the AI suite and select your preferred LLM provider—including OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude. Simply input your API key. If keys are missing, the system gracefully falls back to our local, built-in offline mock engine to keep the client UI unblocked.
