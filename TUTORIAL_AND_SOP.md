# Agency Nexus: Official Tutorial Script & Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

This document is the operational backbone of your agency. It contains an immersive "walkthrough" tutorial script to train new staff and highly granular SOPs to ensure every member of your agency—from the owner to the client—operates at peak efficiency by utilizing our **Unified AI Copilot Engine**.

---

## 🏗 PART 1: The "Agency OS" Master Tutorial
*Use this script for internal onboarding or as a guide for your first 30 minutes in the system.*

### 1. The Command Center (Dashboard)
"Welcome to your new HQ. The **Agency Nexus Dashboard** is where your day starts.
- **Quick Nav:** Use these tiles to jump between departments (Leads, Projects, Messaging).
- **Daily Briefing:** (Admin only) Check this widget first. It summarizes overdue tasks, fresh leads from the last 24 hours, and urgent social interactions tagged with high negative sentiment by the AI Copilot.
- **Project Health:** Look at the 'Project Profitability' widget to see if your margins are healthy across all active accounts in real-time."

### 2. The Sales Pipeline & AI Scoping (EngageTrack & SmartOnboard)
"When a prospect enters our world:
- **Lead Scoring:** Don't chase every lead. Priority goes to scores > 70. Read the AI-generated Lead Summary to understand their pain points before the call.
- **Nurture:** Click 'Email Lead' and use a **Canned Response** or let the AI Copilot suggest a personalized, context-rich response to book a call.
- **Scoping:** Open the **Scope Builder**. select the service (e.g., 'Web Design'), choose the 'Scale' (Small/Medium/Large), and add 'Addons' (e.g., Logo Design).
- **AI Deliverables Polish:** Click '✨ AI Rewrite' in the proposal view. The Copilot instantly converts rough scope bullets into professional, high-ticket deliverables and terms.
- **The Close:** Click 'Generate Proposal' to send an interactive, e-signable document. When signed, AutoPilot triggers project kickoff."

### 3. Execution, AI SOPs & Delivery (Projects & ContentMatrix)
"Now we fulfill the promise:
- **Tasking:** Every project must be broken into tasks. Assign them to specialists.
- **AI SOP Generator:** When creating a task, click the '🪄 Generate AI Brief' button. The Copilot generates a comprehensive, step-by-step SOP inside the task description, giving our team a perfect execution roadmap.
- **Dependencies:** If the Developer can't start until the Designer is done, link the tasks using the 'Depends On' field.
- **Content Architect:** For content-heavy clients, use the **Pillar Architect** to map out 'Topic Clusters' visually, then click '🪄 Generate Content Outlines' to have the AI write draft skeletons for each cluster node.
- **Gantt Visualization:** Use the visual timeline to spot bottlenecks before they delay a client deliverable."

### 4. Client Experience & AI Chat (ClientSync & ApprovalFlow)
"We never use email for project details. Email is for marketing; Agency Nexus is for delivery.
- **Communication Hub:** All chat happens here. It's real-time, branded, and secure. Use the '✨ AI Polish' assistant to ensure your replies are professional and clear before hitting send.
- **Version Control:** When a draft is ready, upload it. The system archives the old version so the client can use the 'Side-by-Side Compare' tool.
- **One-Click Sign-off:** The client portal allows for instant approvals. If they request changes, highlight their feedback and click '✨ AI Improve' to apply edits instantly."

### 5. Financial Mastery & AI Overdue Reminders (MoneyFlow)
"We track profit, not just revenue.
- **Profitability:** The system uses `Budget - (Team Hours * Hourly Rate) - Expenses` to show your 'True ROI' in real-time.
- **Automated Billing:** Send a branded invoice. If they are late, click '🪄 Draft AI Reminder'. The Copilot writes a custom payment reminder based on the invoice terms and the client's relationship history."

## 📋 PART 2: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### SOP: Level 1 - Admin / Agency Owner
**Goal:** Strategic scaling, AI optimization, and oversight.
1.  **Daily Review:** Check the 'Daily Briefing' at 9:00 AM. Assign any unassigned leads and review AI sentiment alerts on social channels.
2.  **AI Audit:** Every Monday, navigate to **Nexus > Settings > AI Copilot** to verify API key connectivity, model quotas, and logs. Transition between OpenAI, Gemini, and Claude depending on current model costs and analytical needs.
3.  **Referral Hub:** If a team member's capacity is > 90% (visible in TimeBlock Pro), delegate overflow projects to a 'Referral Partner' in BurnoutGuard.
4.  **Financial Audit:** Every Friday, review 'Financial Reports'. Investigate any project with a margin < 30%.
5.  **Automation Audit:** Once a month, review **AutoPilot** rules. Ensure webhooks and triggers are firing smoothly.

### SOP: Level 2 - Team Member / Staff
**Goal:** Productive execution, time-tracking, and AI-powered collaboration.
1.  **Task Management:** Check your 'Active Tasks' dashboard upon login. Prioritize 'High' and 'Urgent' items.
2.  **AI Onboarding:** When assigned a new task type, use the **AI SOP Generator** in the task brief to understand best-practice execution steps.
3.  **Time Tracking:** Log your hours *immediately* after completing a task. Notes should be client-facing quality (e.g., use the '✨ AI Polish' button on your time entry description).
4.  **AI Smart Scheduling:** Click '🪄 Generate Smart Schedule' in TimeBlock Pro every Monday morning to let the AI build an optimized focus schedule based on your deadlines.
5.  **Focus Sprints:** Use **Focus Mode** for tasks requiring deep concentration. Avoid checking DMs during these intervals.
6.  **Burnout Check:** Log your stress level every Friday in **BurnoutGuard**. If you are at a '10', the Admin is automatically alerted.

### SOP: Level 3 - Client (Onboarding instructions for your clients)
**Goal:** Streamlined collaboration and fast approvals.
1.  **Centralized Chat:** Please send all project-related questions through the **Messages** tab in your portal. This ensures our whole team stays updated.
2.  **Deliverable Review:** When you receive a 'Pending Approval' notification, visit the **Approval Portal**. Use the 'Compare' view to see improvements from previous versions.
3.  **Sign-off:** Please Approve or Request Changes within 48 hours to prevent project delays. Use the inline annotation tools if you need small copy edits.
4.  **Payments:** All invoices are available in your portal. You can pay via Credit Card or PayPal for instant receipt.
5.  **Resource Library:** Check your 'Resources' tab for your brand guides, onboarding docs, and signed contracts.

## 🗺 PART 3: The 90-Day Agency Scaling Roadmap

Adopting a new operating system takes time. Follow this roadmap to transition your agency from "Tool Chaos" to "Calibrated Scale."

### Phase 1: Days 1-30 (The Foundation)
*   **Goal:** Centralize data, configure AI keys, and standardize onboarding.
*   **Weeks 1-2:** Clean your existing database. Enter your current clients and active projects. Configure your AI keys under settings to unlock the **AI Copilot**.
*   **Weeks 3-4:** Build your interactive **Scopes** for your top 3 service packages. Use the **Proposal Generator** and **AI Proposal Rewriter** for all new leads.
*   **Milestone:** All new business is captured, scoped, and AI-drafted inside Agency Nexus.

### Phase 2: Days 31-60 (The Execution)
*   **Goal:** Drive team adoption, smart scheduling, and financial transparency.
*   **Weeks 5-6:** Train team members on the **Project View**. Mandate that all labor is logged, and use **AI Smart Schedules** in TimeBlock Pro to budget hours.
*   **Weeks 7-8:** Configure **SMTP** and start using **ClientSync** with AI reply suggest for all client communication. Move one existing client into the portal.
*   **Milestone:** Your dashboard shows real-time **True Profit** and AI-driven schedules for at least 50% of active projects.

### Phase 3: Days 61-90 (The Optimization)
*   **Goal:** Automation, AI-driven operations, and sustainable growth.
*   **Weeks 9-10:** Audit your workflows and create your first 5 **AutoPilot** rules. Integrate AI automated content outlines in ContentMatrix.
*   **Weeks 11-12:** Monitor **BurnoutGuard** metrics. Use the **Referral Hub** to offload low-margin or overflow work to partners.
*   **Milestone:** You are managing your agency from a single tab. Context switching is reduced by 80%.
