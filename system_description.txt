================================================================================
                       AGENCY NEXUS SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

Agency Nexus is an enterprise-grade, all-in-one Agency Operating System (Agency OS)
designed natively for WordPress. It is specifically engineered to centralize,
streamline, and automate the entire lifecycle of freelancers, solo agency owners,
and scaling digital teams. By consolidating ten crucial business departments into
a single, cohesive, high-performance command center, Agency Nexus eliminates the
"Tab Tax" (unnecessary multi-tool SaaS subscription fatigue) and drives peak
operational efficiency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE ARCHITECTURAL MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   💼 SmartOnboard (Client Onboarding & Deals):
    Features an Interactive Scope Builder that standardizes services and tiers.
    It performs an exact pricing computation breakdown (Project Scale base + Addon
    Total = Subtotal - Special Terms discount = Final Total Price). Its built-in,
    highly intelligent Special Terms parser dynamically detects flat discounts
    (e.g., "$500") or percentage discounts (e.g., "10%"), subtracting them
    exactly and recording the breakdown within the sent proposals.

*   📅 ContentMatrix (Content Planning & Creation):
    Enables visual drag-and-drop editorial scheduling across multiple channels,
    fully optimized for PHP 8.1+ with robust type-safe date handling. It features
    the Pillar Content Architect to organize content into visual topic authority
    clusters and the AI Batch Automation tool to draft dozens of skeletons in seconds.

*   ✅ ApprovalFlow (Posting & Sign-off):
    A streamlined client review portal with side-by-side draft comparison engines,
    revisions tracking, and annotations, preventing endless email loops.

*   🧠 EngageTrack (Lead Intelligence & CRM):
    Captures leads through optimized forms with built-in redirect builders. It
    calculates strategic lead value scores (0-100), offers AI summaries, and
    aggregates interactions from Instagram, LinkedIn, X, and Facebook with AI
    interaction intent and sentiment analysis.

*   💰 MoneyFlow (Financial Intelligence):
    Tracks labor costs against team hours and expenses to calculate the exact,
    real-time "True ROI" of every project. Generates professional invoicing
    with custom logos and automated 5% late fees.

*   💬 ClientSync (Communication Hub):
     Branded, secure, real-time client-staff chat with shared resource storage
     and role-based visibility.

*   ⏰ TimeBlock Pro (Time & Schedule Management):
    Built-in Focus Mode Pomodoro timer, Office Hour boundary enforcements with
    automated responders, and AI Smart capacity scheduling suggestions.

*   🏗 FreebieFactory (Resource Library):
    Vault for swipe files, contract clauses, and discovery questionnaires
    with built-in AI optimization.

*   🤖 AutoPilot (Automation Center):
    A flexible conditional automation system triggering actions (Zapier, Slack,
    email briefings, or SOP creations) when events occur.

*   ❤️ BurnoutGuard (Health & Sustainability):
    Workload and stress monitors for team members, vacation calendars, and
    referral networks to safely delegate excess projects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. NATIVE UNIFIED AI COPILOT ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The cognitive backbone of Agency Nexus is a native, context-aware AI Copilot Engine
capable of interfacing seamlessly with top LLM providers (OpenAI ChatGPT, Google
Gemini, and Anthropic Claude).

*   SEO-Optimized Refinement:
    The "✨ AI Improve" link next to ContentMatrix Titles and Body Content fields
    sends specialized prompts to optimize titles (limited to 60 characters, catchy,
    and CTR-focused) and refactor draft copies into robust, comprehensive article
    templates featuring proper headings (H2, H3), paragraphs, lists, and keyword
    flows.

*   Loose Field & Context Matching:
    The central AJAX handler matching engine processes fields loosely. Both
    'body', 'content', 'description', and 'notes' fields seamlessly route to
    the article optimizer, while 'title' fields route to the headline refiner.

*   Elegant Local Fallback & Branding:
    For local development or when API keys are absent, the system degrades
    gracefully to the offline Local CoPilot mock engine. It dynamically retrieves
    the selected default provider from the settings and customizes mock signatures
    (e.g., branding responses as 'OpenAI ChatGPT Response' instead of static text)
    and weaves user inputs (e.g., "Testing consulting business") directly into
    premium, bespoke SEO article mockups.
